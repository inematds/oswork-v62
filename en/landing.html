OSWork v6.2 · AI as a work system

Your AI needs a system

From chat to your agent environment. You organize files, teach procedures to the AI, and build routines you can verify—one short lesson at a time.

start now →

8 modules · lessons of ~15 minutes · complete material for each topic at the end of the lesson · module 1 available

What you’ll be doing

Choose the model for the taskwith a written quality ruler before the request.
Set up your workstationfolders, instructions, Skills, and memory that the AI reads across the whole project.
Operate with safetyCodex, Git, a bot on Telegram, and a supervised service on a VPS.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if

  • You already use an AI chat at work and want to go beyond casual conversation.
  • You want to learn the technical side—terminal, Git, server—explained step by step.
  • You prefer to learn by doing, in a training folder.

It’s not for you if

  • You only want quick request tips, without touching files (see OSWork v6).
  • You want a traditional video lesson.
